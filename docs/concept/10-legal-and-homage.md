# 10 — Legal & Homage Guidelines

> Not legal advice. These are the project's own rules to stay clearly on
> the side of an original fan homage.

## Licences
| What | Licence | File |
|---|---|---|
| Source code, build scripts, shaders, tests, tools | **MIT** | `LICENSE` |
| Art & content: textures, sprites, voxels, models, maps, music, sound, story text, concept art, fonts made for the project | **CC BY 4.0** | `LICENSE-ART` |
| Third-party libraries | their own (all permissive: zlib, MIT, PD) | `CREDITS.md` |
| Third-party assets (if any) | CC0 or CC BY only (no NC/ND/SA to keep the content licence simple) | `assets/ATTRIBUTION.md` |

- Every contributor agrees that code they contribute is MIT and art is CC BY 4.0
  (stated in `CONTRIBUTING.md`, to be added with the first code).
- Every asset has an attribution entry; CI checks for missing entries.
- Generated assets (AI tools, procedural) are allowed only if the tool's terms
  permit the CC BY 4.0 release; mark them as such in the attribution file.

## Homage rules
What is **copyright-protected** are concrete expressions: art, sprites,
models, sounds, music, text, code, level layouts, characters. What makes a
game **recognisable** to fans is often its **mechanics, genre conventions and
mood**, which we may borrow. **Trademarks** (game names, logos, character names)
must not be used as if they were ours.

So:
1. **Do borrow:** mechanics (shrink-and-stomp, skiing, heat management,
   reactor countdown, conversion, chronoshift), genre conventions (keycards,
   buy menus, ring menus), moods and palettes, structural ideas (a hub with
   episode gates), jokes in the spirit of the original.
2. **Don't copy:** any asset (not even placeholders), music or melodies, sound
   effects, voice lines, exact quotes longer than a short nod, logos, UI art,
   level layouts (no "dust2 remake"), character designs.
3. **Names:** in-game names are original puns (*Disc-o Launcher*, *Tinselium*,
   *Over-Mince*, *Baublegum City*). Real trademarks (*Doom*, *Quake*, *Half-Life*, …)
   appear only in documentation and the credits' "inspired by" list, never in
   titles, menus, or store pages.
4. **Characters:** all original. Silhouettes and roles may nod to the
   original (a silent observer, a bald prophet, a rogue AI), but designs,
   names and dialogue are ours.
5. **Extra care for big franchises:** for day 17 (*Jedi Knight* / Star Wars),
   avoid all franchise terms, sounds and designs (no "Force", no "lightsaber",
   no "Jedi"; our terms: *Spirit*, *candlesaber*, *Order of the Twelve
   Nights*). The same applies to Warcraft/StarCraft race names, C&C faction
   names, etc.
6. **Real-world weapons** in day 7 get fictional, punny names.
7. **No monetisation**: the project is free and non-commercial; no ads, no
   donations tied to the homages.
8. **Disclaimer** in the credits: "This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan
   homage. All inspiring games and their trademarks belong to their owners.
   No affiliation or endorsement."

## Personal data
- The personalisation pack (friends' nicknames and photos) is **not
  committed** to the public repository and not deployed to the public
  `gh-pages` site. Load it from a local file or a private URL.
- No analytics, no tracking. Playtest timing logs are local and opt-in.
- The relay stores no data beyond live room membership.
