# Day 18 — Three Choirs
*Homage to **StarCraft** (Blizzard, 1998)*

## The original
- **Gameplay:** sci-fi RTS with three **truly asymmetric** races:
  - **Terrans:** humans with bunkers, **siege tanks** (deploy into siege mode
    for long range), marines with **stim packs** (faster, costs HP),
    ghosts with cloaking and nukes, buildings that lift off.
  - **Zerg:** a swarm that builds on **creep**, spawns from larvae,
    **zergling rushes**, **burrow** to ambush, overlords for supply.
  - **Protoss:** psionic aliens with **regenerating shields**, buildings
    that need the power field of **pylons** ("You must construct additional
    pylons"), **psionic storm**, warp-in.
- **Look:** 2D sprites with vivid team colours, space platforms, alien
  worlds, glowing effects.
- **Memorable:** "Zerg rush", the siege tank's *thunk*, psi-storm, the
  campaign that moves from race to race.

## Homage concept
Three peoples have to sing together for the **Carol of the Stars** to ring
again: the **Tinkerans** (human engineers in mechs), the **Swarmlings**
(sugar-bug swarm — infected by KRAMPUS.EXE) and the **Prismoss** (crystal
elves with psionic shields). Like the original's campaign, the level is
played in three acts, each with one people's tools.

**Glitch Lieutenant:** the **Over-Mince**, a giant brain-shaped mince pie
that controls the swarm (cure it, don't kill it; it's a victim too).

## Signature mechanics
1. **Act 1 — Tinkerans: Stim & siege.** The *Stim Shot* doubles speed and fire
   rate for 10 s at a cost of 10 HP. Deploy the *Siege Cannon* (your
   portable turret) into siege mode for long-range artillery; man a bunker
   with marine allies.
2. **Act 2 — Swarmlings: creep & burrow.** "Frosting" creep spreads over the
   ground: it slows you and heals swarm units; burn it with the flamer.
   Burrowed ambushers pop out when you walk over them (motion tells).
   Waves of **Sugarlings** rush you (a "zergling rush" set piece).
3. **Act 3 — Prismoss: shields, pylons & psi-storm.** You get a regenerating
   shield on top of health. Place **pylons** to power doors, bridges and
   turrets in their power field — a puzzle. The **Psi-Storm** spell hits
   everything in an area. NOEL (or a Prismoss adviser): "You must construct
   additional pylons."

## Enemies
- Swarmlings: **Sugarlings** (fast), **Spitters**, **Burrowers**, **Ultra-licks**
  (huge armoured bugs), **Overlords** (floating, spot you).
- Corrupted Tinkeran turrets; the **Over-Mince**.

## Level flow (≈20 min)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Briefing: a three-panel portrait briefing with three advisers. |
| 0:30 | **Act 1 (Tinkerans):** a mining outpost; bunker defence; stim; siege cannon on a ridge vs. waves. |
| 6:00 | **Act 2 (Swarmlings):** into the hive; creep, burrowers, a Sugarling rush down a canyon, the Ultra-lick. |
| 12:00 | **Act 3 (Prismoss):** a crystal temple; shield; pylon puzzles; psi-storm on a swarm. |
| 16:00 | **Finale:** the Over-Mince: power three Prismoss purifiers with pylons while holding off the swarm with siege cannon and storm. Cured, the swarm goes quiet. Bauble. |

## Secrets & challenge
- Floppies: in a Tinkeran building that lifts off (fly up on its roof); under
  burrowed creep; a hidden pylon slot that powers a secret door.
- **Golden Joystick:** "GG no re": finish act 2 without taking creep damage.

## Look & sound
- Palette: Tinkeran rust and steel blue; Swarmling purple-brown with pink
  icing; Prismoss gold and cyan glow.
- Team-colour palette swaps on sprites; glowing pylon fields; psi-storm with
  dozens of small dynamic lights.
- Music: a different style for each act: Southern rock, organic drums, choral.

## Multiplayer
- Co-op: up to 4. Versus (bonus): "Three Choirs" — 3 teams, one per
  people, king-of-the-hill.
