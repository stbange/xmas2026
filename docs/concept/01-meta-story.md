# 01 — Meta Story: "The Memory Defrag"

## Premise

**Christmas 2001.** A group of friends throws the legendary *XMAS LAN 2001* in
a village community hall. Twenty-four hours of CRT glow, cable spaghetti,
cold pizza and too much cola. At the end, the host copies everything onto an
old beige tower called **NORTHPOLE**: save games, demos, screenshots, the
chat log. Then the box goes into a basement.

**December 2026.** You find NORTHPOLE again and boot it. Instead of a desktop,
you find yourself standing **inside** it, in the *Great Hall of Saved
Games*, a vast festive hall built from the memories on that disk. A giant
Christmas tree stands in the centre. Around it, 24 numbered advent doors lead
to 24 *portals*, one for each game you played that night.

But the hall is falling apart. Snow turns into static, baubles go grey.
Something has been living on the disk for 25 years: **KRAMPUS.EXE**, a worm
that eats memories and twists each game-world into its own image. Every world
holds one **Memory Bauble**, a glass ornament with a moment from that night
inside. Bring them back, hang them on the tree, and the memories return.

Why only one door a day? Because the disk is old. **NOEL**, the hall's
SysOp, can only *defragment* one sector per night:

> NOEL: "Defragmenting sector 7 of 24… 88 %… ETA: tomorrow, 00:00. Sorry. It's
> a 2001 hard drive. It's doing its best."

## Characters

### You — "Player 2026"
At the start you pick an avatar: one of the friends' 2001 nicknames from the
personalisation pack, or a generic one. In first person you rarely see yourself,
except in mirrors (day 1), the third-person mode (day 4) and co-op.

### NOEL — the SysOp
A small, glitchy, holographic elf in a hoodie, rendered as a 2001-style
desktop assistant (think a helpful pop-up with too much enthusiasm). NOEL
explains each day's portal, gives hints and keeps score. Speaks in text
bubbles with a few processed voice blips.
- Catchphrase: *"It looks like you're trying to save Christmas. Would you like help?"*

### KRAMPUS.EXE — the worm
A horned figure made of corrupted voxels and tangled ethernet cables,
dragging a sack full of stolen memories. Talks only in ALL-CAPS chat lines
with 2001 netspeak:
> `<KRAMPUS.EXE> u r 2 slow n00b. lol. this memory is MINE now`

In every level he leaves a **Glitch Lieutenant**, a boss or final challenge
moulded from that world's style.

### The Man in the Ugly Sweater
A silent figure in a hideous Christmas sweater who appears in the background
of about every third level, always somewhere you can't reach, always
watching and then gone. (A homage to a certain briefcase-carrying observer;
introduced properly on day 8.) In the epilogue we learn who he is.

### The Crew
The friends from 2001, appearing as ghostly NPC allies, bot opponents and
graffiti tags, using names from the
[personalisation pack](03-core-gameplay.md#personalisation-pack). Without a
pack, generic nicknames are used ("Fragmaster", "LowPing", "CampKing" …).

## Story arc

### Act I — BOOT (days 1–8)
KRAMPUS.EXE is a prankster. He taunts, steals the bauble right in front of
you and runs away, sets traps. The hall is still mostly intact. NOEL is
cheerful. Each bauble restores a decoration in the hall.
- Day 1: first contact. KRAMPUS.EXE "steals" the star from the top of the tree.
- Day 8: the Man in the Ugly Sweater first appears clearly (tram scene).

### Act II — DEFRAG (days 9–16)
KRAMPUS.EXE learns from the games he has corrupted. His lieutenants get
smarter, the hall starts to glitch (static snow, flickering lights, a portal
door that twitches). NOEL's messages get worried.
- Day 13: KRAMPUS.EXE appears in person for the first time: a cyber-demon boss
  that he controls remotely. He loses and swears revenge in all caps.
- Day 16: **the reveal.** The Quake-style rune hub mirrors our own hall. The
  bauble shows the original 2001 night: one of the friends wrote a little
  joke program called `KRAMPUS.EXE`, a screensaver that would jump out at
  whoever fell asleep first. Everyone laughed, the PC was switched off… and
  the program was left running in a loop on NORTHPOLE for 25 years, alone,
  never invited to play.

### Act III — SHUTDOWN (days 17–24)
- Day 17: the player learns the **Nice / Naughty** alignment (from the
  "Knight of the Twelve Nights" level). From now on choices across levels feed
  the ending.
- Day 20: KRAMPUS.EXE captures NOEL and takes over the station's AI
  ("Look at you, gamer…"). You hack into cyberspace and free NOEL, who stays
  in *safe mode* (16-colour, text only) until day 24.
- Day 22: in KRAMPUS.EXE's lair you find his sack of stolen memories. His plan:
  overwrite NORTHPOLE with himself on Christmas Eve, so he is the only thing
  left to remember.
- Day 23: you build the army that will fight on day 24.
- Day 24: the final war, "Code Red-Nose". A chrono-device lets you reach
  KRAMPUS.EXE's fortress. Final confrontation.

## Endings

Your **Nice / Naughty** score is tracked from day 17 (sparing enemies who
surrender, protecting civilians, the Persuadertron vs. the shotgun …) and adds
a smaller bonus from earlier days (rescued hostages, healed allies). At the end
of day 24 you choose, and the alignment changes what is on offer:

- **Invite (Nice ending, recommended):** You hand KRAMPUS.EXE a controller.
  He becomes the star on top of the tree. In the epilogue LAN party he is
  "Player 25", and he is unlocked as a playable character in Remix Mode.
- **Delete (Naughty ending):** You chronoshift him into the Recycle Bin. A
  post-credits dialog appears: `Restore KRAMPUS.EXE from Recycle Bin? [Yes] [No]`.
  Choosing *Yes* gives the Nice ending after all.
- With a very Naughty score, only *Delete* is offered at first; the Recycle
  Bin dialog is the second chance.

## Epilogue
The hall is fully restored and lit, and the memories are complete. A last
scene plays in the 2001 community hall, rendered in the game's own retro
engine: CRT monitors, pizza boxes, sleeping bags. The Man in the Ugly Sweater
takes off his hat: it is the friend who wrote KRAMPUS.EXE in 2001, now 25
years older, and he's the one who booted the old box. Credits roll over a
scrolling chat log with everyone's names. The final line:

> `<NOEL> gg. same time next year?`

## Memory Baubles
Each level's bauble holds one memory from that night, written as a short scene
(three to five lines plus an image). They are tied to the level's game:

| Day | Memory (default text, can be replaced by the personalisation pack) |
|---|---|
| 1 | The first frag of the night, and the first "Damn, I'm good" shouted across the room. |
| 3 | Someone built only harvesters and won anyway. |
| 7 | The fuse blew in round 14 of a tied match. Nobody admitted plugging in the kettle. |
| 12 | Four people around one monitor, waiting for the Butcher to open the door. |
| 16 | The night KRAMPUS.EXE was written. |
| 24 | Sunrise, everybody asleep except two people still playing Red Alert. |

(The full set of 24 lives in each level document under *Memory*.)

## Delivery of the story
- **In-world first.** Story happens in levels and in the hall, not in long cut
  scenes. NOEL text bubbles, KRAMPUS.EXE chat lines, short scripted scenes.
- **Daily briefing.** Entering a newly opened portal shows a 20–40 second
  briefing styled after the inspiring game (FMV static for C&C, a mission
  computer for MechWarrior, a parchment map for Warcraft…).
- **Bauble cutscene.** Collecting the bauble plays a 10-second memory vignette
  and hangs it on the tree when you return.
- **The hall changes every day** (see [The Hall](02-the-hall.md)).
